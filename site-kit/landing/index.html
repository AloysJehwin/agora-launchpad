One call. Four things happen.

  1. You get a call

    Leave your number. Seconds later your phone rings and a voice agent says hello.

  2. You talk

    It asks a few questions about your business. Answer the way you would tell a customer.

  3. You watch your site appear

    A preview link arrives on WhatsApp. Open it and the page builds section by section. Ask for a change out loud and it is applied in seconds.

    • “Make it green”
    • “Add my WhatsApp number”
    • “Also in Tamil”

  4. It goes live

    When you are happy, say so. The agent publishes your site at its own address.
